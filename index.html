About The Fest

Empowering the next generation of quantum developers

Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of worldwide events hosted by university students and researchers focused on quantum computing. Pragati Engineering College is proud to host this premier celebration, bringing together brilliant minds to learn Qiskit, build real circuits, and compete.
